Dupahiya Season 2 review: still warm, still funny, just not quite as effortless as the first run

By Milind Patil4 min readPrime Video · Hindi Series · Audience-First Review

Official platform artwork used as the hero image.

If you liked the first season's village-chaos charm, Dupahiya Season 2 is still an easy enough recommendation. The warmth is intact. The ensemble still works. The world is still inviting. The difference is that this season feels a little more crowded and a little less clean in how it lands its best ideas.

MyMovieJam verdict: if you enjoy heartland ensemble comedy with social mess, local politics, and characters who feel lived-in rather than manufactured, Dupahiya Season 2 is a solid 8 / 10. It is watchable, likable, and still easy to recommend to the right viewer.

Why it works

The main strength is still the world. This is the kind of show where place does a lot of the work. The village setting does not feel like wallpaper. It feels like the engine. That helps the comedy because even smaller incidents carry gossip value, emotional spillover, and consequences that make the world feel socially alive.

The other win is the ensemble rhythm. Even when the plot gets bumpier, the people keep the season moving. You are not watching only for one twist. You are watching because the interactions themselves are enjoyable enough to sustain the ride.

What viewers are actually saying

  • The positive lane is clear: people still like the warmth, the local texture, and the easy familiarity of the world.
  • The recommendation is not blind: even the viewers who enjoyed it often admit this season is messier or less sharp than the ideal version of itself.
  • The honest read: this lands more in the “good company” zone than the “drop everything now” zone, which is still a healthy recommendation lane.

Watch if

You liked Season 1, enjoy village-world dramedy, and want something warmer and more character-driven than the average streaming thriller.

Skip if

You need tighter plotting, sharper comic precision, or a sequel season that clearly levels up from the first one.

Audience pulse

The broad reaction feels healthy: people still like spending time in this world even when the season is not operating at peak sharpness.

Who should watch it

  • Viewers who liked Season 1 and want to revisit Dhadakpur with the same cast energy.
  • Prime Video watchers who prefer warmth and ensemble texture over prestige-drama polish.
  • Anyone who likes village dramedies where the world and the people matter as much as the plot.

The honest caveat

This season sounds and feels like a show carrying slightly more than it can always balance. The warmth is there. The charm is there. But some of the comic rhythm and plotting seem less effortless this time.

That matters because Season 1 built a certain expectation: that the show could feel cozy and controlled at the same time. Season 2 still gives you the cozy part. It just does not always give you the same consistency.

Bottom line

If you are asking whether Dupahiya Season 2 is worth your time, the answer is yes — especially if the first season already put you in its lane. Just go in expecting a strong return, not a giant leap.

The independent MyMovieJam score is 8 / 10. That means we recommend it, but with the honest note that the setting and characters are doing more of the heavy lifting than perfect execution.
