Anaesthesia on Netflix review: Polish viewers keep recommending Leszek Lichota’s medical drama, with a gore warning

MyMovieJam scoreRating card →7.9/10Good
By Milind Patil4 min readNetflix · Polish Medical Drama · Audience First

Anaesthesia (Polish title Znieczulenie) is Netflix’s first Polish medical drama. Leszek Lichota plays Dr Dawid Łazarz, a top neurosurgeon whose career ends after a risky operation fails. He takes a job at a county hospital in Suwałki, where his habit of breaking rules to save patients wins him fans and enemies. All eight episodes, about 40 minutes each, arrived on November 4, 2026. It is a Netflix original with no earlier TV run.

It went to No. 1 on Netflix in Poland within days, and Netflix ordered season two on November 17. The better sign is that people were still telling friends to watch it more than a week after launch.

MyMovieJam verdict: if you want a tense, fast hospital drama with a strong lead, Anaesthesia is a solid 7.9 / 10. Lichota is the reason to press play. If you are squeamish about surgery or want strict medical realism, look elsewhere.

What viewers are actually saying

We read the reactions on X and Reddit before scoring this. X is where the love is. Most of the posts we found are plain recommendations, mostly in Polish, and they kept coming from November 13 to 17. Reddit is thin. We found only a couple of one-line mentions, and the Polish-language threads we found on r/Polska and r/poland were removed by moderators. Quotes in Polish are translated by us.

  • @wilkolak_sport on X (translated): “I started at 8 and finished in the night. It was great to watch. The action keeps you in suspense, and Leszek Lichota was brilliant as usual… I recommend it.” Post
  • @FryzjerMurana on X (translated): “Honestly, I don’t like Polish productions, but ‘Znieczulenie’ pulled me in for a few good hours in front of the TV… I recommend it right away.” Post
  • @eli__witch on X (translated): “Grey’s Anatomy, The Pitt and Dr House in one. If you haven’t seen it yet, I recommend it!” Post
  • @smorgasbordgie on X: “Anaesthesia (Znieczulenie) on Netflix is really good, if you’re into Polish medical dramas.” Post
  • r/netflix: “Anaesthesia…..Polish doctor. Excellent” Thread
  • A Filmweb viewer, quoted by RMF FM (translated): “Finally a Polish series with real tension, where you don’t have to be embarrassed for the actors.” Article

Not everyone is sold. One X user called it decent but “not quite the level” of Ślepnąc od świateł (post, translated). Another loved it but said it “ended so strangely” that they miss the rest of the story (post, translated). A third warned that if you dislike seeing blood, brains and broken bones, “maybe don’t watch” (post, translated). Plotek also ran a piece in which doctors pick apart some of the surgery scenes (article). The pushback is real, but the recommendations clearly outnumber it this week.

Watch if

You want a fast, tense hospital drama with a strong lead performance, and you are fine with graphic surgery scenes and strong language.

Skip if

You are squeamish about blood and surgery, want strict medical realism, or need a tidy ending; some viewers wanted more from the finish.

Audience pulse

Warm and steady on X, almost all of it plain recommendations. Thin on Reddit. The complaints are gore, medical realism and an ending that leaves some people wanting more.

Why it works

The comparison viewers reach for most is House, with Grey’s Anatomy and The Pitt close behind. That is the pitch: a rule-breaking surgeon, a small-town hospital and cases that keep moving. Several viewers say they finished it in one or two sittings, and one watched four episodes straight.

Almost every positive post names Lichota. Viewers who had seen him in Znachor say he is just as good here. The Polish viewers also keep saying the series feels like a proper local production, not a copy of an American show.

The honest caveat

It is graphic. Viewers warn about blood, brain surgery and broken bones. A parents guide we checked also flags strong language and drug use, and says the sexual content is moderate and not explicit. It is listed 16+ in Poland.

If you want strict medical accuracy, skip it. And because most of the praise comes from Polish viewers, there is less English-language Reddit chatter to lean on than for most picks we publish. That is why the score stops at 7.9.

Bottom line

If you want one non-English series to start this weekend, start with Anaesthesia. Most people who write about it recommend it, and they say so within days of finishing. The ones who don’t mostly wanted less gore or a fuller ending.

The independent MyMovieJam score is 7.9 / 10. We recommend it, with a clear warning about the surgery scenes.
