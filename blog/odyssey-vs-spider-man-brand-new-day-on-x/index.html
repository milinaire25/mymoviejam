Odyssey vs Spider-Man: Brand New Day on X: which audience is hotter right now?

Published · August 8, 2026 By MovieJam Crew 5 min read

Hero image built from official poster art for both films.

If you ignore critics for a second and just watch what regular people are actually saying on X, the split between The Odyssey and Spider-Man: Brand New Day is not love versus hate. It is love versus a slightly different kind of love. One is getting the giant-screen, “I need to see that again in IMAX” kind of reaction. The other is getting the “that made me feel like a kid again” crowd-pleaser surge.

That matters because a lot of moviegoers are not asking, “Which movie is more important?” They are asking, “Which one is more likely to work on me tonight?” That is the better question, and the X chatter gives a pretty clear answer.

MyMovieJam quick take: The Odyssey is currently winning the prestige-awe lane on X, while Spider-Man: Brand New Day is winning the emotional superhero lane. Our current independent audience-sentiment read lands at 9.3/10 for The Odyssey and 9.0/10 for Brand New Day.

The Odyssey

9.3 / 10

People are talking about scale, repeat IMAX trips, and “best movie of the year” energy more than almost anything else.

Brand New Day

9.0 / 10

People are talking about emotion, Tom Holland's reset, and how satisfying it feels as a big, broad, rewatchable Spider-Man yes.

What X is saying about The Odyssey

The loudest Odyssey posts are not just saying it is good. They are saying it feels massive. That is an important difference. The audience language around it leans toward awe, scale, immersion, and format obsession. You see a lot of “go IMAX,” “best of the year,” and “I had to go back” style reaction. That is not normal background approval. That is event-cinema behavior.

The upside of that kind of reaction is obvious: if you want a movie that feels like you are leaving the house for something genuinely big, The Odyssey has the stronger public signal right now. The only caution is that this is a more self-consciously grand experience. If you are not in the mood for ambition and size, the same qualities other people love can feel heavier to you.

What X is saying about Spider-Man: Brand New Day

Brand New Day is getting a different kind of heat. The posts around it sound more personal, more nostalgic, and more instantly shareable. People are emphasizing how it made them feel, how much they liked Peter Parker being grounded again, and how easy the movie is to recommend even if you are not trying to overthink the larger MCU situation.

That is why this movie reads as the safer broad-audience pick. The X conversation has more “I loved this,” “favorite of the year,” and “I want to see it again” energy than “admire it from a distance” energy. In plain terms: Brand New Day looks like the easier crowd-pleaser, while The Odyssey looks like the stronger big-screen flex.

Pick Odyssey if

You want scale, formal ambition, giant-screen spectacle, and a movie people are treating like a full event instead of a casual Friday watch.

Pick Brand New Day if

You want emotional payoff, a cleaner superhero yes, and the easier answer for a group that just wants a satisfying night out.

The real split

Odyssey wins the awe lane. Brand New Day wins the warmth-and-rewatchability lane.

Fast recommendation: if your real goal is “give me the movie that feels biggest,” pick The Odyssey. If your real goal is “give me the movie I am least likely to regret recommending to other people,” pick Spider-Man: Brand New Day.

Bottom line

These are two successful movies for a reason, but they are succeeding through different emotional promises. The Odyssey is selling magnitude. Spider-Man: Brand New Day is selling connection. X is reflecting that split very clearly. One movie is getting treated like a cinematic achievement. The other is getting treated like a crowd-high that reminded people why they like Spider-Man in the first place.

If you want my cleanest independent audience-first answer right now: The Odyssey is winning slightly overall on raw X prestige heat, but Spider-Man: Brand New Day may still be the better pick for more people on a random night. That is the difference between the “bigger” win and the “easier” win.
