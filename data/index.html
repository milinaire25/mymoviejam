MyMovieJam ratings dataset

Every score on the MyMovieJam ratings index, as free, reusable data: title, year, type, genre, platform, score, band, Watch if and Skip if, and the link to the rating page. It is available as CSV and JSON under a CC BY 4.0 license.

Updated Nov 1, 2026 · Edited by Milind Patil

Download CSV Download JSON

Fields

FieldDescription
titleTitle as shown on its rating page.
yearRelease year as shown on the rating page.
typeFilm, Series, Film/Series or as shown on the rating page.
genreGenre as shown on the rating page.
platformStreaming platform (Netflix, Prime Video, Apple TV+, Disney+, Theatrical). Left blank where we do not track one.
scoreMyMovieJam score out of 10, identical to the score on the rating page (or in the blog review where one exists).
bandGreat (8–10), Good (7–7.9), Mixed (5–6.9) or Skip (below 5).
watch_ifOne-line “Watch if” from the rating page.
skip_ifOne-line “Skip if” from the rating page.
rating_page_urlCanonical URL of the title’s MyMovieJam rating page.

The files carry no viewer counts or review counts; we do not track a panel size per title.

Method

Scores come from a panel of friends and family who watched each title, cross-checked against sentiment on X, with Milind Patil making the final call. Scores are out of 10. Bands: 8–10 Great, 7–7.9 Good, 5–6.9 Mixed, below 5 Skip. Where a MyMovieJam blog review exists for a title, the score in that review is the source and the rating page shows the same number.
